I build machine learning models that drive business decisions, and I care just as much about whether we can tell they worked.

PhD in quantitative methods · former Data Scientist at Loblaw Digital · now completing an MS in Computer Science & AI at Boston University · Seattle area

Background

Industry. I spent three years as a Data Scientist at Loblaw Digital in Toronto, the e-commerce arm of Canada's largest grocery retailer. I designed, built and ran customer models in production on GCP, along with the batch prediction and evaluation pipelines around them.

Research. I did my PhD in Psychometrics & Quantitative Psychology at Fordham (2023), focusing on causal inference and propensity score methods. Before that, I led the statistical analysis and writing for six peer-reviewed journal articles in applied psychology, health and education.

Now. I'm in Boston University's MS in Computer Science & AI (expected December 2027), adding systems, cloud and deep learning foundations to a modelling and statistics background.

What I work on
Causal measurement. Incrementality, uplift, experiments and quasi-experiments: separating what a model predicted from what an intervention actually changed.
Applied ML on behavioural data. Propensity, ranking and segmentation models for customer decisions.
Production ML. Reproducible pipelines, honest evaluation and monitoring that tells you when a model stops working.
